# Exec guide: updating the website

Everything on the website is managed from the **exec dashboard**. You don't
need any technical knowledge. If you can fill in a form, you can update the
site.

**Dashboard:** https://hbu-club.vercel.app/admin
(There's also an "Exec login" link at the bottom of every page.)

Sign in with the shared exec password. Ask the president if you don't have it.
Please don't share it outside the exec team.

---

## The basics

- **Your changes go live as soon as you save.** There's no separate
  "publish" step.
- **Previews:** most edit pages have a **Live preview** on the right showing
  exactly how your changes will look on the website.
- **Saving:** the save button is at the bottom of each form. A green message
  appears when it's saved. If something is wrong, the field is highlighted in
  red with an explanation, and nothing you typed is lost.
- **"Unsaved changes"** appears next to the save button if you've edited
  something but haven't saved yet.
- **Formatting text:** bigger text boxes have buttons for **bold**,
  *italics*, links, and bullet lists. Leave a blank line between paragraphs.
- **Drafts:** most things have a **Show on website** switch. Turn it off to
  keep something hidden while you work on it.

## The dashboard

The first page you see after signing in. The **To do** list shows what needs
attention, for example a month with no history facts, execs without a
headshot, or past events that need a recap. Click any item to go straight to
the fix.

## Events

**Events → New event.** Fill in the title, start time, and location. Optional
extras:

- **Ends:** shows a time range like "6:00 – 8:00 PM".
- **Chapter:** pick the chapter running it, or leave it as "All chapters".
- **Theme:** a short tag like "Local history".
- **RSVP link:** adds an RSVP button (e.g. a Google Form).
- **Cover image:** a poster or photo. Landscape works best.

**After an event**, open it again and write a few lines in **Recap** (how many
came, what happened). It shows in the past-events archive. The dashboard
reminds you about events without one.

**Recurring events:** open an existing event and click **Duplicate**. The
copy starts hidden, so change the date and turn on **Show on website**.

The events list has three tabs: **Upcoming**, **Past**, and **Drafts**.

## History facts

Facts appear in the **"Today in history"** section of the home page and on
the **History** timeline page.

- **When did it happen?** Fill in as much as you know: year, month, day.
  For dates before year 1, choose **BCE / BC** in the Era box.
  - A fact with a **month** is shown on the home page during that month.
  - A fact with a **day** too is featured as **"On this day"** on that date.
- **Headline:** optional, but makes the fact stand out.
- **Region:** e.g. "Canada", "South Asia". Visitors can filter the timeline
  by region.
- **Source:** where the fact comes from. Please add one. We're a history
  club, so accuracy matters.

The row of month boxes at the top of the facts page shows how many facts each
month has. **Yellow boxes are empty months.** Try to keep a few facts in
every month, and add next month's facts before it starts.

## Team

Each exec has a profile on the **Team** page.

- **Headshot:** click the box or drag a photo onto it. Portrait (vertical)
  photos look best. Large phone photos are fine; they're shrunk
  automatically.
- **Name, pronouns, role:** the pronouns and role boxes suggest common
  options as you type, but you can write anything.
- **Chapter:** the exec's chapter, or "Organization-wide" for the leadership
  team.
- **Studies:** program, year, and school appear as one line under the name.
- **Bio:** a few sentences in their own words.
- **Links:** optional email, Instagram, and LinkedIn icons on the card.

**Changing the order:** on the Team list, use the ▲ ▼ arrows next to each
person. Order is kept separately within each chapter.

**When someone steps down:** don't delete them. Open their profile and turn
off **Current exec**. They'll move to the "Past executives" section with
their term (e.g. "2025–26").

## Chapters

Each chapter gets its own page with its team, events, and contact info.

- **Web address:** the end of the chapter's link, e.g. `mcmaster` gives
  `/chapters/mcmaster`. It's filled in from the name if you leave it blank.
- **Chapter email / Instagram** and **where & when the chapter meets** show
  on the chapter's page.
- Turn off **Show on website** while a new chapter is still being set up.
- Deleting a chapter keeps its execs and events. They just become
  organization-wide.

Use the ▲ ▼ arrows to change the order chapters are listed in.

## Site content

Everything else on the home page:

- **Home page text:** the big heading, the small line above it, and the
  intro paragraph. Clearing a field brings back the original text.
- **Sign-up link:** where the "Join us" buttons go (e.g. a membership form).
- **Announcement banner:** a dark bar across the top of every page, good for
  "Exec applications are open!". Clear it to remove the bar.
- **Contact & social links:** shown in the "Get involved" section and the
  footer. Choose the type (Email, Instagram, Discord…) and type the address,
  handle, or link. Something like `@ourhandle` works.
- **What we do:** the numbered cards on the home page. Three works best.
- **FAQ:** questions new members often ask. The section disappears if empty.

## Good to know

- Changes appear on the website immediately. If you don't see them, refresh
  the page.
- Deleting asks for confirmation and **can't be undone**. When in doubt, turn
  off **Show on website** instead.
- Sign out on shared computers (bottom of the sidebar).
- If something looks broken, take a screenshot and message the website
  maintainer.
